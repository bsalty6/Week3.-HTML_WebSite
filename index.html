Justin Jefferson

The 28 year receiver was drafted in 2020 and has quickly become the current BEST wide receiver in the league. He is the the quickest to hit 8,500 yards in his career and is on pace to be the first to 10,000. He is my current all time favorite player and is the reason i feel in love with Football.

Harrison Smith

His nickname is "Hit Man" and plays safety. Still currently active after being drafted in 2012. He has played for the Minnesota Vikings his while career and has been a cornerstone of their defense. He is undoubtably (in my opinion) in conversation to be one of the best safeties in NFL history, easily Top 5.
